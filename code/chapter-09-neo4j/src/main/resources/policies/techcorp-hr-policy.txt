TechCorp HR Policy Manual — Version 2024.1

=== ANNUAL LEAVE ===

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.
Leave must be approved by your line manager at least 5 business days in advance.
Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over to the following year.
Leave encashment is not permitted except upon resignation or termination.

=== SICK LEAVE ===

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry over to the following year.
Extended illness beyond 10 days is covered under TechCorp's income protection policy.

=== PARENTAL LEAVE ===

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Leave may commence up to 4 weeks before the expected due date.
Employees must give at least 8 weeks notice before taking parental leave.
Parental leave can be extended by up to 12 months unpaid upon request.

=== REMOTE WORK POLICY ===

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
At least 2 days per week must be worked from a TechCorp office.
Remote work arrangements are reviewed quarterly.
Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and productive home working environment.
A home office allowance of $500 per year is provided to eligible remote workers.

=== PERFORMANCE REVIEWS ===

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year: in June and December.
Each review includes a self-assessment and a manager assessment.
Ratings are: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement.
Salary reviews are aligned with the December performance cycle.
Employees rated Needs Improvement twice consecutively enter a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

=== LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT ===

Every employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,500.
This budget covers courses, conferences, certifications, and books.
Requests must be submitted through the TechCorp Learning Portal.
Time off for approved training is granted without impacting annual leave.
TechCorp offers an internal mentorship programme matching junior and senior staff.

=== CODE OF CONDUCT ===

All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect.
Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.
Violations should be reported to HR or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.
TechCorp has a zero-tolerance policy for fraud, bribery, or corruption.
Employees must keep confidential company information secure at all times.

=== IT AND EQUIPMENT ===

New employees receive a laptop on their first day from the IT department.
Standard issue laptops are MacBook Pro (engineers) or Dell Latitude (operations).
Peripherals (monitor, keyboard, mouse) are available upon request.
Personal devices used for work must be registered with IT Security.
All company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled.
Employees must not install unlicensed software on company devices.

=== ONBOARDING ===

New employees complete a structured 30-day onboarding programme.
Week 1 focuses on company orientation, tools setup, and meeting the team.
Weeks 2–4 involve shadowing, hands-on projects, and completing mandatory compliance training.
Each new hire is assigned a dedicated onboarding buddy for the first 90 days.
Onboarding is managed through the TechCorp HRIS portal.

=== EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT ===

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
Receipts are required for all expenses above $25.
Travel bookings must be made through the approved TechCorp travel portal.
Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved for a client entertainment event.
Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of approval.

=== HEALTH AND WELLBEING ===

TechCorp provides private health insurance for all employees from day one.
An Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free confidential counselling.
Employees have access to a subsidised gym membership scheme.
Mental health days are included within the sick leave entitlement.
A wellbeing allowance of $300 per year is available for fitness or mindfulness activities.
