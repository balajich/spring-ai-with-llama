🎛️ **"Why does the AI give me a different answer every time?"**

Not a bug — a setting. Chapter 2 of "Spring AI with Llama" goes under the hood of the four concepts that separate developers who fight the AI from those who control it: **tokens, the context window, prompts/messages, and temperature**.

Then we put them to work: `/hr/ask/precise` at temperature 0.0 for policy facts that must never vary, and `/hr/ask/creative` at 0.9 for brainstorming — same model, same code, two very different personalities, tuned with `ChatOptions`.

Bonus intuition: 128K tokens ≈ a 350-page novel. That's the model's whole working memory, and your prompt *and* its reply both spend from it.
