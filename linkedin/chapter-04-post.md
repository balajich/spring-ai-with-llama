📝 **If your prompts are built with string concatenation, you have a bug waiting to happen.**

Chapter 4 of "Spring AI with Llama" replaces hardcoded prompts with `PromptTemplate` — inject the employee's name, department, and role as parameters instead of gluing strings together.

The result is prompts that are reusable, testable, and reviewable, plus a system message that gives the assistant a consistent TechCorp persona across every endpoint.

Prompts are code. Treat them like it.
