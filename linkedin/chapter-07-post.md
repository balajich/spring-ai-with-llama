📚 **Your AI isn't lying. It's guessing.**

Ask a general model about *your company's* parental leave policy and it will invent something plausible — because it has never seen your handbook. Chapter 7 of "Spring AI with Llama" makes it read the manual instead.

That's RAG: embed your policy documents into a vector store, retrieve the relevant chunks at question time, and hand them to the model as context. `QuestionAnswerAdvisor` wires the whole thing into `ChatClient` in one line.

Same model. Same question. Grounded answer — because now it's reading, not remembering.
