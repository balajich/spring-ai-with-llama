🚀 **You don't need an OpenAI account to build AI into Java.**

Chapter 1 of "Spring AI with Llama" builds a working AI Q&A endpoint from scratch — Spring Boot, Spring AI's `ChatClient`, and a Llama 3.2 model served locally by Ollama. Three lines of fluent API and you have a `/hr/ask` endpoint answering employee questions.

The whole thing runs on your laptop. No API keys, no cloud bills, no data leaving your machine — which matters a lot when the questions are about HR policy.

It's the first chapter of a series that builds one real app, SmartHR Assistant, all the way to RAG, tool calling, MCP, and vision.
