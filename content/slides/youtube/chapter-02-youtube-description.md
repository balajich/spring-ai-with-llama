🎛️ Spring AI with Llama #2 — Core Concepts: Tokens, Context Window, Prompt & Temperature

Why does the same question give you a different answer every time? And why is one reply two sentences and the next six paragraphs? We go under the hood: tokens, the context window, prompts/messages, and temperature - the four concepts that separate developers who fight the AI from those who control it.

Then we put them to work: /hr/ask/precise for short deterministic policy answers, and /hr/ask/creative for open-ended brainstorming - both tuned with ChatOptions.

🎯 WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
• What a token actually is (and why 128K tokens is roughly a whole novel)
• The context window - the shared budget for input + output
• How a Prompt is really a list of Messages (System, User, Assistant)
• How temperature controls randomness - and which value to use when
• Capping response length with maxTokens
