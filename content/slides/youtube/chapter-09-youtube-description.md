🕸️ Spring AI with Llama #9 — Graph RAG with Neo4j

Vector search finds similar text. It doesn't find RELATED text. Ask "what happens to my stock options if I go on parental leave?" and pure similarity search returns the leave policy and misses the equity policy entirely - the documents don't sound alike, they're related.

We add Neo4j: embeddings for similarity, plus graph edges between policy sections you can traverse.

🎯 WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
• Where pure vector search falls short
• Neo4j as a vector + graph store
• Building relationships between policy sections
• Cross-topic retrieval with Cypher traversal
