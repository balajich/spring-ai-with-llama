📚 Spring AI with Llama #7 — RAG: Make Your AI Read the Manual

Your AI isn't lying - it's guessing. Ask a general model about YOUR company's parental leave policy and it invents something plausible, because it has never seen your handbook.

RAG fixes this: embed your policy documents into a vector store, retrieve the relevant chunks at question time, and hand them to the model as context. QuestionAnswerAdvisor wires it into ChatClient in one line.
