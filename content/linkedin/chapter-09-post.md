🕸️ **Vector search finds similar text. It doesn't find *related* text.**

Ask "what happens to my stock options if I go on parental leave?" and pure similarity search returns the parental leave policy — and misses the equity policy entirely, because the documents don't *sound* alike. They're **related**, not similar.

Chapter 9 of "Spring AI with Llama" adds Neo4j: embeddings for similarity, plus graph edges between policy sections you can actually traverse. Vector search finds the entry point; the graph walks the connections.

Same `VectorStore` interface, third backend. Cross-topic questions finally get real answers.
