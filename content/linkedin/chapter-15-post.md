🔍 **When keyword search doesn't find what you expect, try semantic search.**

Lisa searched TechCorp's candidates for "backend developer with cloud experience" and got nothing back. Not because nobody fits — four people did. They just wrote it differently: "JVM engineer" and "AWS Lambda", "server-side developer" and "Azure Functions".

Keyword search matches the exact words. Semantic search matches the **meaning** — so it finds those candidates even though their CVs never use the words Lisa typed.

Chapter 15 of "Spring AI with Llama" shows how: turn the resumes into vectors, turn the query into a vector, and return the closest matches. A few lines with Spring AI, all running locally.
