📸 **Your AI bot can talk, remember, and act. Now it can see.**

Chapter 12 of "Spring AI with Llama" adds vision: an employee snaps a photo of a workplace hazard — a trailing cable, a blocked fire exit — and the AI analyses the image and files a structured safety report before they've put their phone away.

One multipart request in, a typed `SafetyReport` JSON out, powered by Spring AI's `Media` API and the LLaVA vision model running entirely on your laptop. Photos of your office and your people never leave your machine — no cloud vision API, no data agreements.

The eleven-field safety form just became "take a photo".
