🧠 **Your AI bot has goldfish memory.**

Ask it a follow-up question and it has no idea what you were just talking about — because every call is stateless. Chapter 6 of "Spring AI with Llama" fixes that with `MessageWindowChatMemory` and `MessageChatMemoryAdvisor`.

Suddenly onboarding works like a real conversation: "What's the leave policy?" → "And for new joiners?" — and the bot follows. Each employee gets an isolated session, and the memory window keeps the token budget bounded so history never eats the whole context.

Memory isn't magic. It's just resending the conversation — carefully.
